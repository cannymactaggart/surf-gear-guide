SECOND-HAND

Buying a second-hand surfboard? Here's what to check.

A cheap board is not a bargain if it has hidden damage or is wrong for you.

Inspect the rails, nose and tail

Look for cracks, major dents, repaired damage and areas that feel soft or unusually flexible.

Check the bottom

Inspect the underside for dents, cracks and repairs. Ask what happened and when repairs were carried out.

Look closely at the fin boxes

Check for cracks around the boxes, movement and signs of previous repairs. Also make sure the fins match the system.

Check for water damage

Water intrusion can add weight and lead to further damage. If something looks suspicious, ask for an explanation before buying.

Make sure the board suits you

Correct dimensions and volume matter more than getting a low price. A board you cannot comfortably ride is poor value.

Before paying: inspect the whole board in good light and ask directly about every repair you can see.
