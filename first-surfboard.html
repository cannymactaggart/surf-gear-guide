BOARD BUYING

Don't buy a surfboard until you've worked out these 5 things.

Choose for the surfer, the waves and the way the board will actually be used.

1. Your ability

A beginner generally benefits from stability, buoyancy and easy paddling. A smaller performance board can make learning harder rather than better.

2. Your size

Height and weight affect the dimensions and volume that make sense. There is no single ideal board length for everyone.

3. Your usual waves

Think about the conditions you actually surf most often. Small, weak waves and powerful waves reward different board characteristics.

4. Board shape

Length is only one specification. Width, thickness, outline, rocker and rails all affect how a board behaves.

5. Your total budget

Allow for fins, a leash, traction or wax and appropriate protection. The board price is not necessarily the complete cost.

Bottom line: the best first board is the one that gives you enough stability and paddle power to catch plenty of waves.
