BUDGET

How much should you actually spend on your first surfboard?

There is no single useful dollar figure. The right budget depends on the board, the surfer and the rest of the setup.

Start with the type of board you need

There is little value in setting a budget first and then choosing a board that does not suit your ability. Work out the type of board before comparing prices.

Compare new and second-hand

Second-hand can offer better value, but condition matters. A damaged or poorly repaired board can become an expensive false economy.

Budget for the whole setup

Remember fins, leash, traction or wax and appropriate exposure protection. The board is only part of the initial cost.

Don't pay for performance you cannot use

A premium shortboard is unlikely to be good value for a beginner who needs stability and paddle power.

Better question: what is the cheapest complete setup that gives this surfer a board they can comfortably ride?
