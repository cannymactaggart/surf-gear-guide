WHAT WE DO

A small Australian surf site with two sides.

Surf Gear Guide is an Australian-focused site about surfing, surf gear and the bits of the surfing world that are simply fun to explore.

We cover everything from choosing your first board and finding the right wetsuit to surfskating, women in surfing and the stranger side of the sport.

The aim is straightforward: useful information when you're trying to work something out, and interesting stuff when you're just here for a look.
