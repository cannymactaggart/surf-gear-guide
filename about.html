ABOUT SURF NUGGETS

Nuggets. No, not those kind of nuggets, although what you do in your own time is up to you. Nuggets of wisdom, nuggets of information, nuggets of advice, nuggets of fun, video nuggets, story nuggets, nugget deals. Surf nuggets.

Surf Nuggets is here to bring useful information to people who surf, want to surf, or simply like the stuff that comes with it. We aim to make surfing and everything related to it a bit less confusing — the boards, the gear, the waves, the etiquette, the slang — all in bite-sized nuggets.

Some nuggets will be practical. Some will be fun. Some may send you down a rabbit hole. Really, you should just put your phone down and go surfing.

Some links on Surf Nuggets are affiliate links, which means we may earn a commission if you buy something through them. It doesn't cost you anything extra.
