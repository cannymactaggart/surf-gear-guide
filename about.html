ABOUT SURF NUGGETS

Surf Nuggets

Nuggets. No, not those kind of nuggets, although what you do in your own time is up to you. Nuggets of wisdom, nuggets of information, nuggets of advice, nuggets of fun, video nuggets, story nuggets, nugget deals. Surf nuggets.

Surf Nuggets is about useful bits of information for people who surf, want to surf, or simply like the stuff that comes with it. We aim to make the confusing bits of surf gear a little easier to understand, without pretending there is one perfect board, wetsuit or piece of equipment for everyone.

Some nuggets will be practical. Some will be fun. Some may send you down a rabbit hole. The point is to make the site useful enough to come back to, whether you're trying to work out what board to buy or just looking for something interesting about surfing.
