ABOUT

Surf gear without the guesswork.

Surf Gear Guide is a practical Australian-focused resource for people trying to work out what surf equipment they actually need and what is worth spending money on.

We focus on buying decisions rather than hype: board dimensions, fin systems, wetsuit thickness, second-hand checks and the small details that can make an expensive purchase a bad one.

Where products are discussed, the aim is to use manufacturer specifications, retailer information and other genuine sources rather than inventing first-hand product experiences.

Our principle: recommend what makes sense for the surfer, the conditions and the budget — not simply the most expensive option.
