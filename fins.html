FINS

FCS, FCS II or Futures? Don't buy the wrong fins.

The board's fin boxes determine which fins will fit.

FCS

FCS uses a two-tab style on many boards. The exact fin and box configuration still needs to be checked before buying.

FCS II

FCS II is a distinct system designed around a different insertion method. Do not assume every FCS-labelled fin or box is interchangeable.

Futures

Futures uses a different single-tab base and box design. Futures fins are not the same as FCS or FCS II fins.

What to check

Look at the board manufacturer's specification or the board listing. If buying second-hand, inspect the boxes for cracks, movement or repairs as well as checking the fins themselves.

Rule: identify the fin system first, then shop for fins compatible with that system.
