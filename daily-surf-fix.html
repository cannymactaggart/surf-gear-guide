FROM THE ARCHIVES · 2007

The Radical Rodents: Australia's tiny surfing mice

Before surfing dogs took over the internet, there were the Radical Rodents. These tiny Gold Coast surfers were trained by Shane Willmott to ride custom-made miniature boards on waves created by passing boats.

Yes, they really surfed. Reports from 2007 describe the mice surfing in Queensland, where passing boats created the waves. Later coverage documented the Radical Rodents riding custom boards and even taking up skateboarding.

The Radical Rodents were already being reported as “the world's smallest surfers” in 2007. Their story is one of those wonderfully strange pieces of Australian surf culture that seems to have disappeared into the old internet.

SURFING DOGS

Some dogs really do surf.

Noosa takes it seriously enough to have a dedicated dog-surfing competition as part of the Noosa Festival of Surfing. Dogs and their humans can compete together, turning something that sounds like an internet joke into a proper surf event.

Want to give surfing a go with your little mate?

Here's some of the kit worth looking at before you head into the water. A properly fitted dog life jacket is a good place to start, particularly one with a handle that makes it easier to help your dog out of the water. And, of course, keep an eye on the conditions and your dog at all times.

If your furry pal turns out to be a natural, you're going to want some evidence. A dog-friendly camera harness such as the GoPro Fetch can let you capture the action without trying to hold a camera at the same time.

And if a little encouragement helps with the training, use whatever small treat your dog already loves. Carrot works for some dogs; there are also plenty of ordinary dog training treats that are easy to carry to the beach.
