FROM THE ARCHIVES · 2007

The Radical Rodents: Australia's tiny surfing mice

Before surfing dogs took over the internet, there were the Radical Rodents. These tiny Gold Coast surfers were trained by Shane Willmott to ride custom-made miniature boards on waves created by passing boats.

Yes, they really surfed. Reports from 2007 describe the mice surfing in Queensland, where passing boats created the waves. Later coverage documented the Radical Rodents riding custom boards and even taking up skateboarding.

The Radical Rodents were already being reported as “the world's smallest surfers” in 2007. Their story is one of those wonderfully strange pieces of Australian surf culture that seems to have disappeared into the old internet.
