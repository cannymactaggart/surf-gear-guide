DAILY SURF FIX

Not everything about surfing needs to be useful.

Interesting, funny, ridiculous or just plain good. Things worth watching, reading and sharing from the surfing world.

FIX #001 · DOGS

Meet Ricochet, the surf dog who had a proper job to do.

Some dogs surf because they can. Ricochet became famous for doing it with a purpose, working with children with special needs, people with disabilities and veterans. Her official YouTube channel has a huge collection of surf-dog footage; this is the one that started the story.

Video: Surf Dog Ricochet's official YouTube channel.

FIX #002 · WIPEOUTS

Everyone wipes out. Some people just do it at Nazaré.

Surfline's Greatest Wipeouts series has been documenting the less glamorous side of surfing for years. This compilation is a good reminder that even watching a wipeout can make a perfectly ordinary beach day feel rather sensible.

Video: Surfline. Their Greatest Wipeouts series includes footage from some of the world's heaviest waves.

FIX #003 · COMPLETELY NORMAL SURFING

When the surf trip includes goats.

Ryan “Hippo” Hipwood joins Jamie O'Brien and friends for a session at Kirra. There is surfing, skydiving and, for reasons that probably seemed perfectly reasonable at the time, a goat boat.

Video: Red Bull / Jamie O'Brien. Found via Boardriding.

FIX #004 · THIS WEEK'S FIND

Surfing dogs are apparently having a moment.

The 10th annual World Dog Surfing Championships took place in California on August 1, 2026, with dogs competing in solo surfing, tandem rides and stand-up paddleboarding. This is exactly the sort of thing Daily Surf Fix was made for.

Read the recent photo feature: Digital Camera World.
