STARTING OUT

What do you actually need to start surfing?

You can spend a surprising amount of money on surf gear before you've worked out what you actually need. Start with the basics and add equipment as it becomes useful.

01

Surfboard

For a beginner, stability and buoyancy generally matter more than having the smallest or most advanced board.
02

Wetsuit

Think location, season, water temperature and fit — not simply the thickest suit you can afford.
03

Fins

Fin size, shape and setup can change how a board feels. The fin box system is a practical compatibility detail, not the main thing to choose by.
04

Leash

Match the leash to the board you're riding. This is basic equipment, not an optional extra.
05

Grip

You need wax, traction or the appropriate combination for your board.
06

Sun protection

Not exciting, but important. Australian UV makes this part of the basic kit.
Before buying anything: if you've never surfed, consider a lesson or hire first. It is a much cheaper way to find out what sort of board and equipment suit you.
