WETSUITS

What wetsuit do you actually need in Australia?

The answer depends on where and when you surf, not simply on buying the thickest suit available.

Start with water temperature

Check the typical water temperature for the area and season you actually surf. Conditions vary substantially around Australia.

Think about wind and time in the water

Wind, air temperature and how long you stay in the water affect how warm you feel. A suit suitable for a short summer session may not be enough for a long winter session.

Thickness is only part of it

Thicker neoprene generally provides more warmth but can reduce flexibility. Suit design, seams and construction also matter.

Fit matters

A wetsuit needs to fit closely enough to reduce water exchange without restricting movement. A poorly fitting thick suit can be less useful than a correctly fitting lighter one.

Rule: choose around your local conditions and actual surfing habits, then compare suits that meet those requirements.
